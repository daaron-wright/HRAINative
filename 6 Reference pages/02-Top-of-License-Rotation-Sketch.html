    How it works

    Height is the level of the work, from 1, graduate, to 6, principal. License is the highest level a person is qualified for. Both are for the workshop to confirm.

    Every move must be a step up for the person and keep every client job covered. The last job is filled from the bench or by a junior hire. A standard optimiser picks the moves; no AI model is used.

    Eligible to move after 12 months on an engagement for junior bands, 18 for middle and 24 for senior. A key person on an account is backfilled with the same skill, and the account lead agrees. Both are placeholders for the workshop to confirm.
